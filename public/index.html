Look at your Fitbit export

Choose the ZIP from Google Takeout. It is read inside this page, on this device. Nothing is uploaded, there is no server, and nothing is stored. After this page has loaded you can switch off the network and it keeps working.

Takeout split into several ZIPs? Select them all at once.

How do I get the ZIP?
  1. Open takeout.google.com, press "Deselect all", then tick Fitbit (newer accounts: Google Health or Fitbit and Google Health).
  2. Choose "Export once", file type .zip, and create the export.
  3. Download it from the e-mail link, then pick it here.

Layouts with JSON files (Global Export Data) and the newer CSV layout (Google Data) are recognised. Files that are not recognised are listed so nothing disappears silently.
